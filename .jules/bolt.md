## 2025-02-18 - [Optimization of length and bit_length kernels]
**Learning:** In `arrow-string/src/length.rs`, replacing `offsets.windows(2)` with a `.zip()` of slice iterators (e.g., `off.iter().skip(1).zip(off)`) significantly improves performance. For `length`, the improvement was ~16.5%, and for `bit_length`, it was a staggering ~67%. This is likely because `zip` of slice iterators better enables the compiler to leverage `TrustedLen` for vectorization compared to `windows(2)`. Additionally, using `ScalarBuffer::from_iter` avoids unnecessary intermediate `Vec` collections when the iterator is already `TrustedLen`.
**Action:** Prefer `zip` of slice iterators over `windows(2)` for performance-critical offset calculations in Arrow kernels. Use `ScalarBuffer::from_iter` for more direct buffer construction.
